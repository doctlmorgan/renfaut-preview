The Renfaut™ Praxis Paradigm

Organizations do not transform simply because they want to.

Organizations can adopt new language, revise policies, and launch equity initiatives while continuing to reproduce the conditions they seek to change. Renfaut™ offers a different way of understanding transformation.

Why Renfaut?

Change can occur without transformation.

Strategic plans, training, listening sessions, new policies, and public commitments may create movement. Yet without shifts in power, relationships, meaning, and organizational conditions, that movement often remains temporary or performative.

Traditional approaches often begin with activity.

  • What initiative should we launch?
  • What policy should we revise?
  • What training should we require?
  • What outcome should we measure?
Renfaut asks a different questionWhat organizational relationships, practices, and structures make equitable thriving possible?

A Praxis Paradigm

Renfaut is an integrated way of knowing, being, and doing.

It helps leaders understand how power and inequity operate, locate themselves within complex systems, and cultivate the conditions through which justice-centered transformation can emerge.

Not a checklistNot a linear modelNot a personality typeNot a quick fix
1

Ways of Knowing

How leaders interpret reality, examine power, and recognize the mechanisms shaping organizational outcomes.

2

Ways of Being

How leaders practice reflexivity, humility, accountability, and relational presence within complex systems.

3

Ways of Doing

How leaders interrupt harm, redistribute power, create new possibilities, and sustain renewal in practice.

Transformation, Not Adjustment

Change modifies what an organization does.Transformation changes what an organization becomes.

Transformation is relational, nonlinear, adaptive, and ongoing. It is not a final stabilized state, but a responsive way of being capable of continual renewal.

Five Interdependent Principles

One living system of transformative practice.

See

Reality Reckoning

Learning to see systems as they truly are.
Imagine

Indeterminate Becoming

Expanding what leaders believe is possible.
Act

Disruptive Engagement

Turning awareness into courageous action.
Shift

Emancipatory Shift

Changing the conditions that shape organizational life.
Renew

Radical Renewal

Sustaining transformation through ongoing regeneration.

The principles are not sequential stages. They operate as mutually reinforcing conditions whose interaction generates transformative potential.

Intellectual Foundations

Built through three critical traditions.

CRT

Critical Race Theory

Makes race, power, history, and structural inequity visible within leadership and organizational life.

CR

Critical Realism

Examines the underlying structures and causal mechanisms that generate the outcomes we can observe.

CT

Complexity Theory

Understands transformation as emergent, relational, nonlinear, and shaped through interacting conditions.

Questions Renfaut Asks

Transformation begins with different questions.

What truths are too costly to speak here?
Who benefits from the current system?
Whose discomfort organizes the system?
Who absorbs the risk of honesty?
What futures are impossible within current conditions?
Where is power being protected through language, process, or civility?
