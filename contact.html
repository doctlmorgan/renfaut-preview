Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the Leadership Lab™ designed for?

Organizations, leadership teams, nonprofit executives, educational leaders, foundations, healthcare organizations, public agencies, and community-centered institutions committed to strengthening equity-centered leadership practice.

How long does a Leadership Lab™ engagement last?

Engagements can range from a focused workshop or retreat to a multi-session leadership development partnership. The structure can be adapted to the organization’s goals and readiness.

Can individuals take the R.I.S.E. Index™?

Yes. Individuals can take the R.I.S.E. Index™ and use the results as the basis for reflection, coaching, or leadership development.

Is the R.I.S.E. Index™ available for teams?

Yes. Teams can use the assessment to support collective reflection, identify patterns across leadership practice, and inform Leadership Lab™ engagements.
