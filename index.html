The leadership challenge
Caring about equity is essential. Knowing how your leadership practice shapes equitable outcomes is the next step.

Leadership intentions matter. So do the patterns, choices, relationships, and organizational conditions those intentions create.

Renfaut™ gives leaders a way to examine that practice without reducing leadership to a personality type or a single score. The goal is not to label you. It is to help you see more clearly—and lead with greater intention.

The Renfaut™ Praxis Paradigm

Five lenses for seeing leadership—and possibility—differently.

R.I.S.E. is grounded in the Renfaut Praxis Paradigm, created by T. Lee Morgan, Ph.D. The five principles give leaders language for examining systems, possibility, action, liberation, and renewal.

Reality Reckoning

See systems and conditions clearly.

Indeterminate Becoming

Work with uncertainty and possibility.

Disruptive Engagement

Move from awareness to courageous action.

Emancipatory Shift

Reshape conditions toward equity.

Radical Renewal

Sustain transformation through renewal.
