Research & Theory

The intellectual architecture of Renfaut™.

Renfaut™ is a praxis paradigm for equity-centered leadership and transformative organizational change. Its research base integrates Critical Race Theory, Critical Realism, and Complexity Theory to explain how leaders learn to see systems, act with courage, shift conditions, and sustain renewal over time.

Critical Race Theory

Renfaut™ begins from the recognition that racialized power is embedded in policies, practices, relationships, and institutional narratives. Leadership practice must therefore confront structural inequity rather than treat disparity as an isolated event.

Critical Realism

Critical Realism helps explain how unseen structures and generative mechanisms shape visible outcomes. Renfaut™ focuses attention on the conditions that produce organizational patterns, not only the behaviors observed at the surface.

Complexity Theory

Complexity Theory positions organizations as adaptive systems. Transformation is not a linear program but an emergent process shaped by relationships, feedback, tensions, and new possibilities.

From theory to practice.

Renfaut™ translates critical theory into a living leadership practice. The five principles and fifteen facets name the capacities leaders need to recognize structural reality, remain open to emergence, interrupt harmful patterns, reorient systems, and renew relational conditions for equity and collective flourishing.

Praxis Paradigm

Renfaut™ integrates ways of being, knowing, and doing. It is not simply a model for organizing concepts; it is a paradigm for reflective, courageous, and equity-centered action.

R.I.S.E. Index™

The R.I.S.E. Index™ operationalizes the Renfaut™ principles through reflective items that help leaders examine patterns in their current leadership practice.

Leadership Lab™

The Renfaut Leadership Lab™ supports individuals and teams in translating assessment insight into sustained leadership practice and organizational change.
