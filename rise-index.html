A leadership profile, not a personality test.

Questions

Common questions.

How long does it take?

The assessment is designed to be completed in one sitting. Most participants should set aside focused time so they can respond reflectively.

Is this a personality test?

No. The R.I.S.E. Index™ focuses on leadership practice, reflexivity, and developmental patterns connected to transformative leadership.

Can my organization use it with a group?

Yes. The R.I.S.E. Index™ can be used with teams as part of leadership development, coaching, or a Leadership Lab™ engagement.

What happens after I take it?

Your results can be used for personal reflection, coaching, team development, or as the starting point for a Leadership Lab™ experience.
